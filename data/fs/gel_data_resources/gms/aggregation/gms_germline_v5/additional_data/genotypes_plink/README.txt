GMS germline common-variant extract (PLINK 1 binary)
=====================================================

gms_common_variants_extract.bed/.bim/.fam

The same SNPs as the main programme's aggV2 extract, for the NHS GMS germline
genomes (data release v5; probands and the relatives sequenced with them).
FID and IID are plate keys; the counted allele (A1) is the ALT allele; sex is
the recorded sex. No QC has been applied.

../PCs/gms_PCs_projected_ancestry.tsv  the genomes projected onto the main
programme's principal components, with ancestry probabilities.
