  FAM DAD      FAM MOM      unrelated               -0.0069    0.0857

DUP is CHILD1 a second time, under another family ID: in a real cohort, a sample sequenced twice, a participant
recruited twice, or identical twins. So DUP is also DAD's and MOM's child and CHILD2's sibling, which the .fam does
not say.

Every other pair is unrelated: kinship near 0 (from -0.020 to 0.016 here). U01 … U99 are unrelated founders. U20 has
a fifth of its genotypes missing, so its pairs have fewer SNPs (NSNP 26,174 instead of 32,809). The full siblings
share both, one or neither of their parents' copies of a stretch of chromosome, in turn, in blocks of about 10 Mb.
