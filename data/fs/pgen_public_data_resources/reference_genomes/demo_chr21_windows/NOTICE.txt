Notices for the files in this folder
====================================

tumor.bam, normal.bam (and their .bai indexes) hold reads of the cell lines HCC1395 (a breast cancer) and HCC1395BL
(B lymphocytes of the same donor), sequenced by the SEQC2 consortium (Sequencing Quality Control Phase 2, led by the
US FDA) and released by NCBI for benchmarking: study SRP162370,
https://ftp-trace.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/ReferenceSamples/seqc/Somatic_Mutation_WG/data/WGS/ .
reference.fa is two windows of chromosome 21 of the human reference GRCh38, from UCSC (hg38). truth.vcf holds records
of the SEQC2 high-confidence somatic SNV and indel truth set v1.2.1.

These are publicly distributed research data. Please cite:

  Fang LT, Zhu B, Zhao Y, et al. Establishing community reference samples, data and call sets for benchmarking
  cancer mutation detection using whole-genome sequencing. Nature Biotechnology 2021; 39: 1151-1160.
  doi:10.1038/s41587-021-00993-6

The files were prepared – the reads of two windows, moved onto the windows, nothing else changed – by the genomics-wasm
project (https://github.com/comparativechrono/genomics-wasm, commit bfacba3: datasets/strelka2/hcc1395), whose
manifest records every source, byte range and step. Where every file came from, and its SHA-256: provenance.json
(in the workbench's data/examples/somatic).


Training simulator: these files come from the Bioinformatics workbench's example data
(comparativechrono/bioinformatics-workbench, data/examples/somatic). They are public data
of the cell lines HCC1395 (tumour) and HCC1395BL (normal) released by the SEQC2 consortium
- not data of any Genomics England participant. In the simulator the reads are delivered as
/genomes/by_date/2016-08-11/HX00100001/LP3000001-DNA_A01 (normal) and
/genomes/by_date/2016-08-11/HX00100002/LP3000002-DNA_B01 (tumour, with the somatic truth set
as its Variations/LP3000002-DNA_B01.somatic.vcf). The reference is two windows of
chromosome 21 (GRCh38): chr21_10572001_10582000 and chr21_21404501_21423500 - in IGV choose
the genome "GRCh38 chr21 windows (training)".
